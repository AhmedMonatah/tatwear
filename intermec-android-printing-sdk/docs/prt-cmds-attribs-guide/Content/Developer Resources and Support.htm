Developer Resources and Support

  • For help with developing applications using the Smart Printing Resource Kit, visit the Developers Forum at community.intermec.com.
  • To review technical information or to request technical support for your Intermec product, visit the Intermec technical knowledge base (Knowledge Central) at intermec.custhelp.com.
  • To download our current manuals (in PDF), visit the Intermec website at www.intermec.com. Select Support > Manuals, and then select the product from the drop-down list.

Contact Intermec Customer Support

  • In the U.S.A. and Canada, call 1-800-755-5505.
  • Outside the U.S.A. and Canada, contact your local Intermec representative. To search for your local representative, from the main Intermec web page, click About Us > Contact.
